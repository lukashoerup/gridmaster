# Licences and provenance

Gridmaster is a commercial game. Everything in the repo that someone else made —
data, maps, fonts, images, sounds, music, code copied in rather than installed —
and everything generated by AI that ships to players is listed here **before**
it is committed. Installed libraries are covered by the dependency approval in
`docs/decisions.md`, not here.

## Rules
- **Allowed:** public domain / CC0, CC BY (with the attribution text below
  copied into the in-game credits), the Copernicus licence, and other licences
  that explicitly permit commercial use and redistribution.
- **Never:** non-commercial licences (CC BY-NC, "research use only", "personal
  use"), share-alike licences on anything we would have to open in return
  (CC BY-SA, ODbL) unless Lukas approves the consequence in writing, and data
  whose terms forbid redistribution (many exchange price feeds).
- **Calibration-only** data (used to tune the simulation, never shipped) still
  gets a row, marked as such — its terms must permit that use.
- **AI-generated content** gets a row too: what it is, which tool, and that it
  is disclosed on the Steam store page's AI-content survey.
