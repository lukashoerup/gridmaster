### Inferences
- **The niche looks busier now than in 2016–2023.** Steam app IDs are issued in sequence. Anchors from the findings above: Power Grid (2983910) launched July 2024 and GridEnergy (5040690) launched September 2026. So IDs above about 4.0M date from roughly late 2025–2026, and at least ten energy-business-named pages fall in that range (ARCWATT, PowerCorp, Prestige Electric Company, Nuclear Engineer Tycoon, 电网大亨, GOBLIN ELECTRIC, Idle Energy, COALCOM, Hakoniwa Electric, Arcana Gas & Waterworks…). Gridmaster would therefore launch into a busier niche than it would have a few years ago, though mostly against very small titles.
- **The mid-core electricity tycoon slot looks thin.** By name and description, most titles found are oil drilling, nuclear-operator, idle/clicker, puzzle or electrician sims. Mid-core renewable or electricity-market tycoons are rare: the descriptions found match only Power Network Tycoon (an engineering sim) and GridEnergy (incremental).
- **Check the name early.** The working title "Gridmaster" collides with an existing Steam app (2678560), and "Grid Master" variants exist too. Check trademarks and Steam search results before any public use.

### Inferences
- **"Real-data" alone is not a unique hook.** A popular-looking freemium title, Energy Manager, already markets real-life weather.
  - Gridmaster's differentiation would have to come from a *combination*: historical European wholesale prices and weather, plus premium single-player depth, plus 1990s→future era progression.
- **Energy Manager's "stock market" is about company shares**, not electricity prices, judging by its description. No verified title describes modelling wholesale power prices, price volatility or negative prices.
- **Power Grid (digital) is the only verified title with European regional maps.**
  - The maps are Germany, France, Benelux, Eastern Europe and Italy.
  - Each is a separate board, so this is not one interconnected multi-country market. That reading comes from the phrase "each map bringing unique gameplay".
- **Positioning map of the verified titles:**
  - Hardcore engineering: Power Network Tycoon.
  - Hardcore operator: Nucleares.
  - Incremental: GridEnergy.
  - Freemium player-vs-player management: Energy Manager.
  - Board-game abstraction: Power Grid.
  - **Unfilled slot** among them: a premium, mid-core, single-player renewable-energy business tycoon with real market dynamics.
